Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendwise collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Spendwise personal expense tracking application, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the service to you. This includes:

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • The expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep your account secure and the app running.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve Spendwise. This includes letting you log and review your expenses, generating spending summaries, maintaining your account, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your personal financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored so that they are available to you when you sign in. We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We recommend keeping your own backups of any information you consider important, as Spendwise is provided as a tracking tool and not as a system of financial record.

4. Third Party Services

Spendwise may rely on a small number of third party services to function, such as fonts or hosting infrastructure. These providers only receive the limited information needed to deliver their part of the service and are not permitted to use your personal financial data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We may update this policy from time to time, and will revise the "Last updated" date above whenever we do.
